Our Story

More than a building full of books — a home for curious minds.

Greenwood Public Library has served the neighbourhood since 1983. What began as a small collection of books has grown into a shared space for reading, learning, and community life.

Our shelves hold stories from every corner of the world. Our rooms welcome students, families, researchers, and anyone looking for a little quiet.

We offer free Wi-Fi, comfortable study spaces, digital resources, and regular events for all ages.

Whether you are searching for a new favourite author or simply need a place to think, there is always a place for you here.
